WinSCP.com is an unmodified, Authenticode-signed upstream console launcher.
The viewer embeds it for EXE-only portable WinSCP installations. It runs as a
separate process and launches the user's selected WinSCP.exe via /consolechild.
The viewer does not embed WinSCP.exe or implement SSH authentication itself.

Matching upstream source:
https://github.com/winscp/winscp/tree/d0b87ea398708ad1cfd254c8126a7603c3efdc3f
Distributed source archive: third-party/WinSCP-6.5.7-source.zip
The full license is in LICENSE.txt. Hashes are in provenance.json.

The console protocol handshake rejects incompatible EXE/helper versions.
When a WinSCP.com is present beside the selected EXE, that helper takes priority.
